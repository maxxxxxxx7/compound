§ 05 – Common Questions
Before you book

The things owners actually ask.

We're not technical. Is this for us?

+

Yes – explicitly. Our methodology is built for owners who run real businesses and don't have time to learn the difference between an LLM and an API. We translate. You make decisions. We build.

How is this different from hiring an agency or a consultant?

+

Agencies optimize for billable hours; consultants for slide decks. We optimize for one thing: a working system in production inside 30 days. We take two clients at a time precisely because we won't dilute attention.

What happens if the system breaks after launch?

+

The system is built to run on its own – documented, monitored, and stable from day one. Every engagement includes thirty days of post-launch support, and from there we stay on as your ongoing partner: tuning, extending, and on call when something needs attention. You get a system that runs itself, with us in the background whenever you want us.

How do you decide which workflow to automate first?

+

Hours saved per week × deploy complexity × business sensitivity. We pick the highest-impact, lowest-risk wedge first – typically something that pays back inside 60 days of going live.

+
